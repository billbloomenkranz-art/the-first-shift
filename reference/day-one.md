# First day
## Arrival — 8:30
First priority for a new arrival: check in at reception. Reception staff issue the visitor badge until the employee badge is ready. After check-in, ask reception for Morgan Lee, the mentor. Morgan introduces the team and shows the assigned desk.
## Morning — 9:00–11:30
Jordan helps with laptop and access. Morgan walks through the office and explains Project Willow. Review the project folder and document statuses. If access is blocked, ask Morgan for the training copy; do not borrow credentials.
## Lunch — 11:30–12:30
Lunch is flexible within this window. Break room is beside reception. No team lunch is mandatory.
## Afternoon — 12:30–3:30
With Morgan, review the training equipment register. Prepare an internal update for one item: identify its tag, source document, revision and unresolved question. Do not invent missing fields. Save the draft in the training workspace, not the controlled project folder.
## Wrap-up — 3:30–4:00
Review the draft with Morgan. Record blockers and owners. Confirm tomorrow's priorities. First-day success means orientation and a reviewed draft or a documented access blocker; not independent engineering approval.
