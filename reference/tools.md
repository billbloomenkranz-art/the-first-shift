## Document portal
Navigate Projects > Willow > Controlled Documents. Use the document register to identify the current released revision. A filename or modification date alone does not establish approval. Ask Sam if status is unclear.
## Training workspace
Navigate Projects > Willow > Training > First Day. Morgan provides the training equipment register. Save your draft as equipment-review-draft with your initials. This is not a controlled deliverable.
## Timekeeping
