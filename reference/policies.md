# Policies
## Release
New hires may prepare internal drafts. Riley reviews technical content. Casey authorizes external release; Sam issues the controlled transmittal. No new hire sends project specifications or drawings externally on their own. Vendor questions go to Morgan for routing.
## Document status
Draft means not released. In Review means awaiting review. Released means approved for its stated purpose only. Superseded means not current. If revisions or statuses conflict, stop using the disputed information and ask Sam; technical discrepancies go to Riley. Do not assume released means approved for construction.
## Safety
Day one is office-only. Site visits require Avery's orientation and Casey's authorization. For an immediate hazard move to safety, alert nearby people if safe and contact emergency services when appropriate. Do not attempt equipment operation or repairs. Report hazards to Avery.
## Information handling
Do not upload company documents to unapproved external services. Do not share credentials. Ask Taylor about employment policies not documented here.
